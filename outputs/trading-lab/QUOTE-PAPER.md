# Quote-based paper execution and cost accounting

The live trial uses accounting version 2 prospectively. Its recorded balance and all historical valuations remain intact. A dated transition event separates the old snapshot assumptions from new quote-based results. The arbitrary $0.005 observation charge is no longer applied to future cycles. Synthetic Research experiments retain their original illustrative cost model.

New paper buys request exact-input Jupiter USDC-to-token quotes for 10 USDC. Only a direct route through the screened pool is accepted. Paper token amounts use the minimum-output threshold rather than the optimistic quoted amount. A fresh reverse quote must lose no more than 3% at its minimum-output threshold. Exit valuations and delayed exits request token-to-USDC quotes for exact recorded base units. Quote requests longer than 15 seconds are rejected; successful quotes are reused for at most 15 seconds within a cycle. Freshness and risk expiry are checked again before booking a buy. Route fees and slippage are embedded in quote output, so another percentage fee is not deducted.

These are read-only hypothetical fills, not simulated or confirmed transactions. Actual transaction landing, MEV, priority fees, account rent and realized slippage remain unmeasured. USDC is assumed worth $1 for this paper account; it does not reproduce SOL-funded wallet execution or stablecoin depegging. Missing routes block entries. Failed exits retain the position and mark it at zero, which can latch the existing risk shutdown. Existing legacy positions retain snapshot valuation semantics until closed. Both comparison accounts share the entry-security gate, position limits and quote adapter; the baseline does not pay AI inference costs.

Connect a Jupiter API key in Wallet execution to enable read-only paper quotes. Connect the model and enter exact model prices per million input, cached-input and output tokens in Connections & setup. Prices are user-configured, not automatically verified. Reported usage is multiplied by those rates, including cached-input discounts and all reported output tokens. This is a usage-priced estimate, not an invoice reconciliation. The conservative per-call reserve is persisted before each call, settled after a response and retained when usage is missing. Unknown or interrupted billing pauses further AI decisions; no automatic retry or refund is assumed. If usage cost exceeds the reserve, new entries halt rather than silently increasing the spending assumptions.

Infrastructure costs are unmeasured and excluded, not declared free. The optional network-cost field is a declared per-fill scenario, not an observed chain fee; zero explicitly excludes it. Historical charges, usage-priced estimates and unresolved reservations are shown in the cost ledger. Quote source evidence is saved alongside paper events, without API keys.

No model or Jupiter key was configured during verification. No paid model requests or real transactions were performed. Offline tests cover quote fields, screened-pool matching, round-trip rejection, conservative amounts, cache expiry, stale evidence, failed-route retention, no snapshot fallback, preserved legacy costs, cached-token pricing, durable reservation settlement and interrupted billing. Authenticated Jupiter connectivity and actual fills remain unverified.
