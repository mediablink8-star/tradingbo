Evidence, thresholds and remaining unknowns
  • No active or unknown mint/freeze authority; legacy SPL tokens only.
  • Pool liquidity at least $100,000 and pool age at least 24 hours.
  • Among the 20 largest accounts: no sampled owner above 20% of total supply; top ten sampled owners no more than 50%.
  • Reject a liquidity drop of 30% or more versus the last observation of the same pool within 24 hours.
  • Real buys must use the screened pool directly and still pass reverse-quote and transaction checks.
These are conservative starter rules, not universal safety thresholds. Pool vaults are included and can cause false rejections. LP locks, creator history, hidden related wallets and future selling remain unverified. Passing means basic checks passed—not safe or rug-proof. Sells are not blocked by this entry screen.
