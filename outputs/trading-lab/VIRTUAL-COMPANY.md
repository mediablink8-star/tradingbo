Ten operational roles span leadership, research, security, investment, risk, finance, execution, operations and governance. Five roles use the existing model review pipeline; the remaining roles represent deterministic services and CEO supervision. No fictional model calls or employee activity is created.

A fixed shared mission prioritizes net capital growth inside unchanged capital, allocation, shutdown and security limits. AI reviews receive the mission, selected priority and available inference budget. Risk vetoes and unanimous approval remain required. Every live five-agent review must fit its conservative reserve inside the daily UTC allowance; per-role checks run again between calls. The cap covers recorded usage-priced estimates plus unresolved/pending reservations. Actual charges can exceed a reserve; existing over-reserve behavior halts further entries. This is not a guaranteed provider billing limit or a cap on synthetic research experiments. Infrastructure costs remain excluded.

Work orders persist in SQLite, route readiness blockers to the responsible role and resolve when the underlying check recovers. The server's monitor refreshes company work orders independently of the browser. Employee cards show duties, AI/service type, status and assigned orders. The CEO briefing includes company name, open orders and remaining daily inference allowance.
